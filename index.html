01 About the Event

TECHVERSE 2026 is a one-day technology and innovation competition organized by the Department of Computer and Information Science.

The event provides students with opportunities to develop technical skills, explore Generative AI, demonstrate practical projects, express creativity, improve communication skills, and learn about current technology trends.

EXPLORE → TechNova + TechTalkX   |   CREATE → PromptX + AI CineVerse   |   INNOVATE → ProjectX Live

02 Objectives

  • Encourage practical and hands-on technology learning.
  • Improve programming, debugging, and logical thinking skills.
  • Introduce students to Generative AI and Prompt Engineering.
  • Encourage creativity through AI-based media production.
  • Provide a platform to demonstrate working projects.
  • Develop communication and technical presentation skills.
  • Encourage innovation and problem-solving.
  • Expose students to emerging technologies and industry trends.

07 General Rules

  1. The event is an in-campus competition for students of the Department of Computer and Information Science.
  2. Participants must complete registration 2 days before the event.
  3. Participants must follow the rules of each individual competition.
  4. Participants must report before the scheduled activity.
  5. Unauthorized assistance or unfair practices may result in disqualification.
  6. Projects and creative submissions must be the participants' own work.
  7. AI tools may be used only where permitted by the respective competition rules.
  8. Participants must maintain discipline and follow departmental instructions.
  9. Judges' decisions will be final.
  10. The rules and instructions announced by the organizing committee for each competition are final and must be followed by all participants.
